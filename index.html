Welcome

Eye care tailored to you.

We’re an independent practice looking after the whole family. Our aim is to give you the best possible vision and eye health assessment, with ongoing care at intervals that are right for you.

We offer a full range of services, including a fully comprehensive eye examination with the latest imaging technology, NHS sight tests and a full contact lens service. We can also help if you have a minor eye problem or injury.

All our staff have safeguarding training and are dementia friendly, and our little team will be your first point of contact whenever you call or visit.

Imaging technology

Seeing what’s under the surface.

Digital imaging can help with early detection of disease, sometimes before there are any symptoms. And because we store your images, we can compare them from visit to visit.

OCT 3D scanner

Examines the retina and the layers underneath the surface. It’s a safe, painless procedure, and small changes between visits can show a possible problem or help us monitor an eye condition.

External eye imaging

We take baseline photographs and use them to monitor external eye disease such as dry eye.

Shared with the hospital

With your permission, we can send your images to the hospital when required.

Urgent eye care

A new eye problem? We can help.

We offer emergency appointments in CUES, the Stockport Community Urgent Eyecare Service. It’s NHS funded and covers recent onset eye and vision problems. We can treat minor eye conditions, and refer more serious problems to hospital with diagnostic images when required.

Urgent symptoms

Please phone us during normal opening hours for advice and triage.

A minor eye problem

Your pharmacist can often provide advice and medication, or you can contact your GP or NHS 111.

When we’re closed

We close on bank holidays and between Christmas and New Year. Between Christmas and New Year we offer an emergency service for existing patients only, so please call for details. You can find other CUES practices at primaryeyecare.co.uk.

A serious urgent problem that cannot wait

Go to Accident and Emergency.

What’s the difference between a private eye examination and an NHS sight test?
An NHS funded sight test is a more basic check, so we strongly advise adding digital imaging, including OCT scans, for £30. Our comprehensive private eye examination costs £65 and includes all diagnostic tests and the completion of any reports or letters.
What is an OCT scan, and does it hurt?
It’s a 3D scan of the retina and the layers underneath the surface. It’s a safe, painless procedure that can help with early detection of disease, sometimes before there are any symptoms.
How often can I have my eyes examined?
The NHS limits sight tests to every two years, unless there’s a problem or a reason to be seen more often, such as a family history of glaucoma. If you’re on Eyeplan, a private patient or a contact lens patient, you can be seen as frequently as you need. If you’d like more frequent eye examinations, please ask us.
Am I eligible for an NHS sight test?
Please call us on 0161 480 6432 and we’ll give you advice on eligibility.
What does Eyeplan cover?
Eyeplan is our eye care plan. It starts from £6.50 per month, covers all professional fees and offers other benefits and discounts. Please ask us for more details.
Can you help with my child’s short-sightedness?
We offer myopia control contact lens and spectacle options for children, with the aim of reducing the progression of myopia as they grow.
Do I need my GP to refer me for urgent eye care?
No. Many patients are sent to CUES by their GP, but you can self-refer. Call us if you have a new eye problem or minor injury and we will try to help. For a serious urgent eye problem that cannot wait, go to Accident and Emergency.
Can you repair my glasses?
Yes, we will repair or reglaze spectacles, and Jenny can carry out minor adjustments or repairs.
